Bottle Math

The deposit is your own money coming back - if you actually take it back.

Boundary: counting arithmetic only Bottle Math multiplies your counts by your deposit rate and subtracts the trip cost you type in. It never tells you whether a trip is worth your time - it only prices the cans and the detour.

The bag truth

87 cans at 5 cents each bring back $4.35. The bag in the garage is a savings account with a handle.

The trip truth

The cans bring back $4.35 against a $3.00 trip - $1.35 ahead. At $2.65 for 20 cans, the detour costs more than the cans bring back.

The stash truth

15 cans a week for 52 weeks is 780 cans - $39.00 at the deposit, or a growing corner of the garage.

Run your numbers
Bottle Math is exact arithmetic over your counts, your deposit rate, and your trip cost - nothing more.
